1. Executive Summary & Narrative Flow
1.1 Storyline Architecture
Narrative Phase
Description & Plot Points
The Setup
Dr. Aditi, a brilliant tech pioneer and AI researcher, has built a super-smart artificial intelligence system (ADI). Just 24 hours before its public launch, she abruptly shuts down the system and vanishes without a trace.
The Mission
Participating teams act as digital forensics investigators. They are granted emergency access to Dr. Aditi’s digital workspace and must follow the encrypted breadcrumb trail she left behind to determine her location and the reason for her disappearance.
 
2.1 Technical Setup Guidelines
Google Site (Landing Page): Represents "Dr. Aditi's Computer Terminal." Contains background lore, rules, and the initial link to the Google Drive.
Google Drive Structure: Contains encrypted folders, puzzle files, media files, decoy documents, and historic revision logs.
Google Forms (Stage Gates): Serves as password verification points. Correct passwords reveal response validation messages containing the URL to the next stage's folder/document.
Access Permissions: Ensure all files are set to Anyone with the link can view. Keep "Editor" access restricted to avoid accidental file deletion or solution leaks.

Puzzle 8 — The Steganography Mask (Image Color Channels / Brightness Adjustment)
Setup
File: Dark_Terminal.png
Players open an image that appears to be almost completely black. At first glance, it looks like a corrupted or empty image.
There is no obvious text.
However, in the bottom-right corner, almost invisible against the background, is a tiny line:
“The screen isn't empty. Your eyes are.”
This encourages players to manipulate the image rather than assuming it's broken.

Hidden Clue
When the image's exposure/brightness/contrast is increased, faint terminal-like text gradually appears:
> SYSTEM RECOVERY
> DISPLAY: FAILED
> MEMORY: INTACT
>
> What remains when the light is gone?
>
> ████████████████████
> SHADOW
> ████████████████████
>
> CORE ACCESS: [REDACTED]
But SHADOW isn't the password.
The important part is that some characters of the apparently corrupted CORE ACCESS line become visible at a particular brightness/contrast level.
For example:
> CORE ACCESS: S H A D O W _ C O R E
The underscore can be represented by a deliberately faint character.
Master Output / Key
SHADOW_CORE
